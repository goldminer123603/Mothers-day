Dearest Mum,

There are no words grand enough to capture everything you are to me — but today, I want to try.

You are the first voice I ever heard, the warmest embrace I've ever known, and the reason I believe the world can be a truly beautiful place. Every lesson you taught me, every sacrifice you made quietly without asking for anything in return — I see it all, and I am so grateful.

You are my safe place, my greatest teacher, and my loudest cheerleader all wrapped into one incredible person. The love you give so freely is the most precious thing I have ever received in my life.

On this special day, I want you to know: you are celebrated, you are cherished, and you are deeply, endlessly, unconditionally loved. 💕

With all my love, always 🌸

A Poem For You
You carried me before I could walk,
You listened before I could talk,
You loved me before I knew what love was —
And you never stopped, just because.
✿

In every wound, you were the balm,
You gave me roots and gave me wings,
The greatest gift a mother brings.
